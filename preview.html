01

AI-Empowered Wireless Sensing AI 赋能的无线感知

Artificial intelligence empowered robust wireless sensing for smart environments and IoT applications. 面向智能家居与物联网场景，研究基于人工智能的高鲁棒无线感知技术。

02

Ultra-Low Power Wireless 超低功耗无线通信

Software algorithms and hardware design for ultra-low power wireless communication systems. 面向超低功耗无线通信系统的算法与硬件协同设计。

Integrated Sensing & Communication 通感一体化（ISAC）

5G / Wi-Fi / Bluetooth / LoRa based integrated sensing and communication (ISAC) technology. 基于 5G / Wi-Fi / 蓝牙 / LoRa 的通信感知融合（ISAC）技术。
